The counter

Square sausage, haggis & a proper scone.

Scottish home baking in the window, a sandwich bar for lunch and breakfast rolls all day. Come early for the best of the trays.

  1. Scones

    Straight from the oven onto trays in the window. Have a fruit scone with butter and jam if you're staying.

    FruitCheeseCherry almond

  2. Breakfast rolls

    All-day breakfast baps with square sausage, haggis and black pudding on the softest roll. Reviewers order the house rolls by name.

    Holy TrinityThe GazScottish sampler plate

  3. Hot pastries

    Flaky sausage rolls, Scotch pies and traditional pasties, all baked in the house.

    Sausage rollScotch pie

  4. Lunch

    Filled rolls, baguettes and toasties that come out piping hot, plus weekly specials with a twist.

    Buffalo chicken toastieEgg salad baguette

  5. Coffee & hot chocolate

    Cappuccinos with a heart in the foam, and a hot chocolate reviewers call delicious.

Vegan options come up in 14 reviews: vegan sausage and vegan haggis rolls are on the board as well.
